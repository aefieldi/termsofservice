2. What this policy covers

This policy covers personal data used in connection with:

  • the Spiritual Business Skool membership hosted at skool.com/amanda;
  • related courses, calls, workshops, and email or other messages with us;
  • this website (the pages on fireyourbossfast.com that publish these policies); and
  • social accounts an account owner authorizes us to access, including through TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API, and through tools such as Composio.

If you only open these pages, this site does not collect personal data through forms, accounts, or tracking cookies. The host that serves the pages (GitHub Pages) may process technical data such as an IP address in its own logs. We do not use those logs to identify you.

3. Data we collect

Account and contact information

Name, email address, and profile details you or Skool provide when you join, purchase, or write to us. If you email us, we keep the message and your email address.

Payment information

Payments are taken by Skool and Skool's payment processors. We do not store your full card number. We may receive limited billing details, such as the fact of a payment, the amount, the product, and whether it succeeded.

Community activity

Posts, comments, and other content you add in the community, membership tier, participation in challenges or workshops, and similar activity needed to run the membership.

Communications

Emails and messages you send us, and service messages we send you about your membership or a purchase.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or non-essential cookies. Skool, and other platforms we use such as Meta, TikTok, and YouTube, may collect usage data and set cookies when you use those services. That collection is described in their own policies. If you interact with our ads on Facebook or Instagram, Meta processes that activity under Meta's terms.

Connected social accounts

When an account owner authorizes access, we may receive data from that account. For TikTok, this can include basic profile information, account statistics, and video lists, and the content the owner directs us to publish. Other platforms the owner connects may provide similar account information that the authorization covers. We do not receive the account password. Authorization happens on the platform's own screen.

4. Purposes and lawful bases

We use personal data for the purposes below. The lawful basis is the GDPR ground that allows that use (Article 6).

Provide the membership and related services

To host your membership, deliver courses, calls, workshops, and AI artifacts, and support you as a member or customer.

Lawful basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

Take payment and keep records

To confirm purchases handled by Skool and to keep the records tax and accounting rules require.

Lawful basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

Operate and moderate the community

To display posts to members, keep the community safe, and act on conduct that breaks the Terms of Service. Members can reasonably expect this. We do not use this basis to send unrelated marketing.

Lawful basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

Service messages and questions

To send messages you need in order to use a membership or purchase, and to answer questions you send us. Messages about a membership you already have are part of the contract. A question from someone who has not joined is handled so we can respond to the request.

Lawful basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b)), or legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) when you are not yet a member.

Authorized social accounts

To manage and analyze an account whose owner has authorized access, and to publish content when that owner directs us to. This is described in the next section.

Lawful basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a)), through the authorization the owner gives on the platform, and contract (Article 6(1)(b)) where the owner has asked us to do this work.

Security

To protect accounts, members, and the service against misuse.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

Optional communications

If we send a message that is not required to deliver a membership or purchase, we will rely on consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time, as described under Your rights. We do not require consent for the ordinary service messages a membership needs.

Lawful basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a)), where used.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we do so only when those interests are not overridden by your rights. You can object as described below.

5. TikTok and other platform API data

We use platform APIs only for accounts whose owners authorize that use. For TikTok, the connection may use TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, including through Composio. The same approach applies to any other social account an owner chooses to connect.

For an authorized TikTok account, the data can include basic profile information, account statistics, and video lists. If the owner directs us to publish, we send the content they ask us to post (such as a video and a caption they supply) to TikTok.

We use this data only to manage and analyze the authorized account and to publish content at the account owner's direction. We use it in line with that platform's terms.

We do not sell this data. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers for targeting.

The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings for apps and connected services, and in the equivalent settings of any other platform they have connected. Revoking access stops further collection. It does not, by itself, delete data we already hold.

The owner can email amanda@fireyourbossfast.com and ask us to delete the platform data we hold. We will delete it without undue delay, unless a legal obligation requires us to keep a limited record. We will tell you if that is the case.

6. Sharing with processors and other services

We do not sell personal data. We share it with service providers who help us run the business. Some process data on our instructions (processors). Others are independent services and, once you use them, decide parts of the processing under their own terms. The services are:

  • Skool, and the payment processors Skool uses: community hosting, membership accounts, and payments.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail and Calendar): email and scheduling.
  • Notion: notes and internal organization of the business.
  • Composio: connecting social accounts that an owner has authorized, so those accounts can be used with automation tools at the owner's direction.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram, including ads): advertising and related business tools.
  • TikTok: the authorization you give, the account data that authorization covers, and content posting you direct.
  • YouTube: video hosting and related features used for the business.
  • Anthropic (Claude), and similar AI providers when used for this work: processing needed to create or assist content and AI artifacts.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting these policy pages.

Content you post in the Skool community is visible to other members. Please do not post anything there that you do not want other members to see.

We may also disclose information if the law, a court, or a regulator requires it, or if it is necessary to protect someone's vital interests or to establish or defend a legal claim.

7. International transfers

We are established in Spain. Some providers above process personal data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. TikTok may also process data in other countries where it operates.

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA and the law requires a safeguard, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision or on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, together with any supplementary measures the provider applies. You can email us to ask for a summary of the safeguards used for a transfer.

8. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, and then delete or anonymize it, unless the law requires a longer period.

  • Membership and community records: for as long as the membership is active, then for up to 24 months so we can answer follow-up questions, handle disputes, and complete deletion. Posts stay until you delete them, you leave and they are removed, or we remove them. Copies in backups are removed on the provider's backup cycle.
  • Connected social-account data: until you revoke access or ask us to delete it, and then without undue delay, unless the law requires us to keep a limited record.
  • Messages: for up to 24 months after the last message in the thread, unless we need a message longer for a legal claim or a legal duty.
  • Payment and accounting records: for as long as Spanish tax and accounting law requires. Accounting records are commonly kept for up to six years.
  • This website: we do not build analytics profiles from visits to these pages.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Erase data, in the cases the GDPR provides;
  • Restrict how we use data, in the cases the GDPR provides;
  • Port data you provided, where the processing is based on contract or consent and is carried out by automated means;
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and object to direct marketing if we ever send it;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before the withdrawal. You can withdraw authorization for a social account by revoking it in that platform's settings, and you can ask us to delete the data we already hold.

To use these rights, email amanda@fireyourbossfast.com. We may ask for information to confirm we are dealing with you. We respond within one month. If a request is complex, the GDPR lets us extend that by up to two further months, and we will tell you if we do.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. Our lead authority is the Spanish Data Protection Agency, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD):

No online service is completely secure. Use a strong, unique password for your Skool account, and revoke a social-account connection you no longer want.
